## 📸 The Ultimate Facial Recognition System

**AI Smart Attendance System** is a completely standalone, proprietary software developed entirely by our in-house engineering team. We built this from the ground up to provide schools, universities, and enterprises with a flawless, high-speed solution for automating attendance tracking using advanced computer vision.

We know how frustrating it is to waste the first 10 minutes of every class calling out names or passing around a sign-in sheet. Standard biometric systems are expensive and slow. Our native engine uses a custom-trained YOLOv8 model and OpenCV to recognize faces in real-time, marking attendance in milliseconds.

It handles multi-face detection, mask-wearing scenarios, and low-light conditions without breaking a sweat. You don't need to rely on manual spreadsheets. This is **our own fully working product**, rigorously tested in high-traffic environments.

> 💡 **Why trust our software?** Because we control the code. Privacy and accuracy are paramount. Our proprietary **"Anti-Spoofing Engine"** ensures that a student cannot hold up a photo or a phone screen to mark attendance for a friend. It requires a live, 3D facial depth check, ensuring 100% accuracy.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work with masks?</b></summary>

Yes. Our YOLOv8 model is trained on a dataset that includes people wearing medical masks. It analyzes the upper half of the face to confirm identity.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is the data stored in the cloud?</b></summary>

No. Privacy is our top priority. All facial data and attendance logs are stored locally in a SQLite database on your machine. Nothing is sent to external servers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can it be fooled by a photo?</b></summary>

No. Our Anti-Spoofing Engine uses a liveness detection algorithm that checks for micro-movements and 3D depth. A flat photo or phone screen will be rejected.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need a high-end GPU?</b></summary>

For a single camera feed, a standard CPU (like an i5) is enough. However, if you want to process multiple camera feeds simultaneously at 60 FPS, we recommend an NVIDIA GPU.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does it cost nothing?</b></summary>

This is our official, free release for the educational community. If someone is selling you an attendance system, they are scamming you with a stolen or outdated script.
</details>
